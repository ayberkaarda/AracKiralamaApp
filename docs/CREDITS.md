# Image credits

The car photographs in `src/AracKiralama.UI/Assets/Cars/` come from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/)
and are used under the free licenses listed below (CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA only). They are **not** covered by the
MIT license of the code: every photograph keeps its own license. Each one was cropped around the car and
downscaled to 640 pixels wide for the application, and some had a licence plate in the background blurred.
Nothing else was changed. The application bundles these files and never downloads them.

Car makes and model names are used only to identify the vehicles shown. This project is not affiliated with
or endorsed by any vehicle manufacturer, and the photographers are not affiliated with this project.

Every photograph under CC BY-SA, as modified here, stays under the same CC BY-SA license as its original (the
version named in its entry), in this repository and in any build of the application. Anyone reusing it must keep
the attribution and the license.
